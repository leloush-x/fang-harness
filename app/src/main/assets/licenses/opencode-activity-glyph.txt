OpenCode Android (github.com/anomalyco/opencode-android), small AgentActivity glyph, license not supplied with the repository; Fang Harness adapts its AGSL shader and visual design for the working/subagent glyphs.

Source: github.com/anomalyco/opencode-android,
feature/session/src/main/kotlin/ai/opencode/feature/session/agentactivity/
(AgentActivityShaders.kt, AgentActivityOrb.kt, ActivityGyro.kt, GlyphModels.kt).

Files adapted:
- app/src/main/res/raw/activity_orb.agls — the AGSL fragment shader, byte-identical to
  AgentActivityShaders.ACTIVITY_ORB_FIELD with only a source header added.
- ui/components/activityglyph/* — a reimplementation of the glyph's per-state dot-field
  simulation (10 AgentActivity states and their designs), with the upstream constants.
